## 🌟 What is Laya?

In Sanskrit musical and yogic traditions, ***Laya* (लय)** signifies rhythm, absorption, and graceful dissolution into harmony. 

**Laya - Snapgrid** is a modern reimagining of the classic 10x10 polyomino block puzzle, crafted from the ground up for deep focus, smooth cadence, and calming satisfaction. Rather than frantic timers and flashing popups, *Laya* rewards patience, intentional placements, and rhythmic combo flow.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧩 1. Dynamic Polyomino Grid Mechanics
- **3 Distinct Grid Sizes:**
  - **8×8 Compact:** Fast, tight, tactical sessions.
  - **10×10 Classic:** The quintessential balanced puzzle arena.
  - **12×12 Extended:** Deep, expansive strategic canvas for marathon sessions.
- **Dual Refill Modes:**
  - **Easy (Casual / Mindful):** 4 tray slots with instant single-slot refilling and a dedicated **1-step Undo** button.
  - **Hard (Strategic / Traditional):** 3 tray slots requiring full batch placement before new pieces are dealt.

### ⚡ 2. The Laya Cadence & Dissolution System
- **Laya Flow Meter:** Maintain a steady rhythm of placements and line clears to build your Flow Multiplier (`x2` up to `x5`).
- **Dynamic Dissolution Bursts:** Cleared rows and columns burst into colorful particle showers matching the exact colors of the placed blocks.
- **Post-Game Board Review:** When you run out of moves, use **"View Board"** to examine your final board state, analyze your placements, and learn for your next session.

## 🕹️ Controls & How to Play

- **Desktop (Mouse):** Click and drag any piece from the bottom tray onto the grid. Fill complete rows or columns to clear them and score points.
- **Mobile (Touch):** Touch and drag pieces with touch-elevation offset for unobstructed visibility under your finger.
- **Undo Move:** Made an accidental placement in Easy mode? Tap **"↺ Undo Move"** in the lower-right corner of the meter to take it back.
- **Settings:** Customize themes, board sizes, sound effects, and haptics anytime from the top-right gear icon.
