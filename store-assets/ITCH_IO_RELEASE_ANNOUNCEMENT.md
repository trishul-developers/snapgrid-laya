## 🌟 What is Laya?

In Sanskrit musical and yogic traditions, ***Laya* (लय)** signifies rhythm, absorption, and graceful dissolution into harmony. 

Most modern mobile puzzle games bombard you with timers, flashing ad banners, lives, and paywalls. We built **Laya** as an intentional antidote:
- **No frantic timers** – play at your own natural pace.
- **No ads or microtransactions** – 100% free and open-source forever.
- **Works 100% offline** – fully cached in your browser and available as a lightweight 3 MB native Android APK.

## ✨ What's In the Game?

### 🧩 1. Three Board Sizes
- **8×8 Compact:** Fast, tight, tactical sessions.
- **10×10 Classic:** The quintessential, perfectly balanced arena.
- **12×12 Extended:** Deep, expansive strategic canvas for marathon sessions.

### 🎮 2. Dual Gameplay Modes
- **Easy (Mindful & Casual):** 4 tray slots with instant single-slot refilling and a dedicated **1-step Undo** button for accidental misplacements.
- **Hard (Strategic & Pure):** 3 tray slots requiring full batch placement before new pieces are dealt.

### ⚡ 3. The Laya Cadence & Dissolution System
- **Laya Flow Meter:** Maintain a steady rhythm of placements and line clears to build your Flow Multiplier (`x2` up to `x5`).
- **Dynamic Dissolution Bursts:** Cleared rows and columns burst into colorful particle showers matching the exact colors of the dissolved blocks.
- **Post-Game Board Review:** When you run out of moves, use **"View Board"** to examine your final layout, analyze your placements, and learn for your next session.

### 🎨 4. Curated Material 3 Palettes
Choose between **Material Pastel**, **Cyber Neon**, **Earthy Terracotta**, **Monochrome**, or use the **Custom Color Studio** to pick your own palette. Every shape receives a balanced 6-color rotation so no single color ever floods the board.

## 🕹️ Controls & How to Play

- **Desktop (Mouse):** Click and drag any piece from the bottom tray onto the grid. Fill complete rows or columns to clear them and score points.
- **Mobile (Touch):** Touch and drag pieces with touch-elevation offset for unobstructed visibility under your finger.
- **Undo Move:** Made an accidental placement in Easy mode? Tap **"↺ Undo Move"** in the lower-right corner of the meter to take it back.
- **Settings:** Customize themes, board sizes, sound effects, and haptics anytime from the top-right gear icon.
