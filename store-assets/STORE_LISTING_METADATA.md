## 5. Store-Specific Listing Guides

### 5.1 Indus Appstore (PhonePe)
- **Portal:** [Indus Appstore Developer Console](https://developer.indusappstore.com/)
- **Binary to Upload:** Upload `app-release.aab` or `app-release.apk`.
- **Primary Market:** India.
- **Key Advantage:** Dedicated tabs for Indian languages. In the console, add localized descriptions in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Kannada to gain prominent editorial featuring in regional app discovery!
- **Permissions Declared:** Only `INTERNET` and `VIBRATE`. No sensitive permissions requested.

### 5.2 Samsung Galaxy Store
- **Portal:** [Samsung Seller Portal](https://seller.samsungapps.com/)
- **Binary to Upload:** Upload `app-release.apk`.
- **Target Category:** `Game` $\to$ `Puzzle`.
- **Age Rating:** `4+` (General Audience).
- **Supported Devices:** Select all Galaxy smartphones & tablets.

### 5.3 Amazon Appstore
- **Portal:** [Amazon Developer Console](https://developer.amazon.com/)
- **Binary to Upload:** Upload `app-release.apk`.
- **Device Support:** Check all Amazon Fire Tablets (Fire HD 8, Fire HD 10, Fire 7) and non-Amazon Android devices.
- **Content Rating:** `All Ages` (Zero violence, zero adult content, zero advertisements).

### 5.4 itch.io
- **Portal:** [itch.io New Project](https://itch.io/game/new)
- **Classification:** `Game`
- **Kind of Project:** `HTML` (Playable in browser)
- **Upload 1 (Web):** Upload `dist/` zipped as `laya-webapp-v0.4.0.zip`.
  - Check: *"This file will be played in the browser"*
  - Embed size: `480` × `850` (Orientation: Portrait)
  - Enable fullscreen button: Yes
- **Upload 2 (Android):** Upload `app-release.apk`.
  - Check: *"Downloadable"* with the **Android** platform icon.

### 5.5 Google Play Console
- **Portal:** [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console)
- **Binary to Upload:** `android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`
- **Data Safety Form:** Select **"No"** to all data collection (zero analytics, zero tracking).
- **Target Audience:** All ages (Everyone).

# Privacy Policy for Laya Snapgrid

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Laya Snapgrid ("the App") is developed as an open-source, ad-free, 100% offline game.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
The App does NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics. All game state, high scores, and customization settings are stored strictly on your local device via local storage.

### 2. Advertising and Third-Party Services
The App contains zero advertisements, zero tracking SDKs, and zero third-party telemetry libraries.

### 3. Permissions
The App requests only:
- VIBRATE: To provide optional tactile haptic feedback during piece drops and line clears.
- INTERNET: Used strictly by the web view wrapper to register the offline service worker cache. No network communication occurs during gameplay.
